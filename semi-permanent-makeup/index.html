A LITTLE HELP BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Brow & Permanent Makeup Questions

Which permanent makeup services do you offer? Is a touch-up included?

We offer hairstroke brows using microblading, powder brows using micropigmentation, and combo brows combining both techniques. Lip blushing and lash line enhancement are also available.

One complimentary touch-up is included with single-colour powder brows, multi-colour powder brows, combo brows and lip blushing. Manual powder brows do not include a touch-up. Please confirm touch-up inclusions for hairstroke brows and eyeliner when booking.

Brow Aftercare

  • Avoid touching or rubbing your brows with your hands or a towel while they heal.

    避免触摸或摩擦：刚做完的区域请不要用手触碰，以免引发感染。避免用毛巾擦拭或搓揉做完的部位。

  • Keep your brows dry for the first 5–7 days. Use a damp cloth to clean around them when washing your face.

    保持干燥：在前5-7天内，尽量避免让眉部接触水。洗脸时，可以使用湿毛巾避开眉部区域清洁脸部。

  • Avoid prolonged direct sunlight for the first two weeks. Wear a wide-brimmed hat outdoors to help protect the colour.

    避免阳光直射：避免长时间在阳光下暴晒，尤其是在恢复期的头两周。建议出门时佩戴宽边帽，以防止色素褪色。

  • Avoid swimming, saunas and heavy sweating while your brows heal.

    避免游泳和桑拿：在恢复期内避免游泳、蒸桑拿或过度出汗，这可能会影响眉部的愈合。

  • Let any scabs fall off naturally. Picking them can remove pigment and affect the result.

    不要撕掉结痂：如果眉部出现结痂，请让其自然脱落。撕掉结痂可能会导致色素脱落并影响最终效果。

  • Keep makeup, skincare and exfoliating products off the treated area until it has fully healed.

    避免使用化妆品和护肤品：在眉部完全恢复之前，请避免在眉部使用任何化妆品、护肤品或去角质产品。

  • Use only the aftercare ointment recommended by your technician, and follow their application instructions.

    使用修复霜：根据技师的建议，使用适当的修复霜涂抹在眉部，以促进愈合。请按照指示的次数涂抹。

  • Avoid strenuous exercise for at least one week to limit excessive sweating during healing.

    避免剧烈运动：在术后的一周内，避免剧烈运动，以防止过度出汗和影响眉部恢复。

  • Book a follow-up visit around 6–8 weeks after your treatment to review the healed result and discuss any touch-ups.

    遵循技师的复查计划：术后约6-8周，建议进行复查，以确保效果稳定并进行必要的补色。
