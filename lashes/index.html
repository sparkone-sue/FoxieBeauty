Lash Questions

Can you help me choose a lash style?

Of course. Whether you prefer natural, wispy, manga or fuller lashes, we can help you choose a style based on your eye shape and preferred look. You’re welcome to bring a reference photo; individual results may vary.

What is your lash refill policy?

Lash refills are complimentary within 7 days, excluding promotional styles. Refills after 7 days and within 14 days cost 50% of the original full-set price for the same style.

After 14 days, please book a new set.

Lash Extension Aftercare

Before Your Appointment

  • Arrive without eye makeup and remove your contact lenses before your treatment.

The First 24 Hours

  • Keep your lashes dry. When washing your face, avoid the eye area.
  • Avoid swimming, saunas, hot yoga and excessive heat.
  • Keep oil-based and alcohol-based products away from your lashes.
  • Gently brush your lashes with a clean lash brush to keep them in shape.
  • Sleep on your back when possible to avoid pressing on your lashes.

预约前准备

  • 到店时请勿化眼妆，并在操作前取下隐形眼镜。

完成后24小时内

  • 保持睫毛干燥，洗脸时避开眼周。
  • 避免游泳、桑拿、高温瑜伽及过热环境。
  • 避免让油性或含酒精的产品接触睫毛。
  • 用干净的睫毛刷轻轻梳理，保持形状。
  • 尽量仰卧，避免压到睫毛。

Daily Care

  • Avoid mascara. Removing it can pull on your extensions and leave product build-up.
  • Do not use eyelash curlers, which can damage your extensions.
  • When touching up eye makeup, gently dab around the lashes with a makeup-remover pad. Avoid dry tissues that may catch on the extensions.
  • Choose a water-based makeup remover and keep oil-based products away from the extensions.
  • Do not pull or pick at shedding extensions. Book professional removal to protect your natural lashes.

日常护理

  • 避免使用睫毛膏，卸除时容易拉扯嫁接睫毛，也可能留下产品残留。
  • 不要使用睫毛夹，以免损伤嫁接睫毛。
  • 修改眼妆时，用沾有卸妆液的化妆棉轻轻擦拭睫毛周围，避免使用容易勾住睫毛的干纸巾。
  • 选择水性卸妆产品，避免油性产品接触嫁接睫毛。
  • 不要拉扯或自行拔除脱落中的嫁接睫毛，请预约专业卸除，以保护天然睫毛。
