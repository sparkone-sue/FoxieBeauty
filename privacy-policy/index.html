Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains privacy considerations when visiting the Foxie Beauty website and using its links to other services.

No customer-information collection on this website

This website is for browsing our services, prices, photos and location. It has no customer account, enquiry form, newsletter sign-up or on-site booking form, and does not directly collect customer names, email addresses or payment details.

Bookings and contacting the salon

Online booking links open Fresha, where booking information and deposit payments are handled separately from this website. Please review Fresha’s privacy information when booking.

If you choose to email or call the salon, the information you share is provided through those channels, rather than collected through a form on this website. Please do not send credit-card details by email.

Maps, external links and technical information

This website embeds Google Maps and links to services including Fresha and Instagram. Loading an embedded map can share technical information, such as your IP address and browser information, with Google. These providers apply their own privacy policies.

The website’s hosting provider may process technical request information when delivering pages and keeping the service secure. This version of the website does not include an advertising tracker, analytics script or an on-site registration form.

Information held by other services

For questions about information entered in Fresha, use Fresha’s privacy and account tools or contact the salon for help. Information shared by email or phone is separate from browsing this website.

Contact

For privacy questions, email foxiebeautyshop@gmail.com or call 437-985-2808.

Updates

We may update this policy as the website or our services change. The revision date appears at the top of this page.
