A LITTLE HELP BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful answers, so you can arrive ready to enjoy your visit.

Still have a question? Email us ↗
01VISITING USWhere is Foxie Beauty located?

You’ll find us at 4750 Yonge Street, Unit 155, North York, near Yonge & Sheppard. We offer nail, lash and permanent makeup services by appointment.

02BOOKINGDo I need an appointment? How do I book?

Yes, we operate by appointment. Book online through Fresha and pay your deposit by credit card to secure your visit. Only the deposit is paid online; the remaining balance is due at the salon after your service.

03MANICUREWhat does the $60 manicure include?

Our Single-Colour Structured Gel Manicure includes nail shaping, cuticle care, a structured gel base and single-colour gel polish. Nail art and nail-tip extensions are available at an additional cost.

04NAIL DESIGNHow much are nail art and extensions?

Nail art is added to the $60 manicure, with prices varying by design and the number of nails decorated. French tips, cat eye, ombré and chrome finishes start at $2 per nail.

Nail-tip extensions are an additional $30 for a full set, or $3 per nail. Please see our service menu for other design add-ons.

05PEDICUREWhat is included in a pedicure?

Our Gel Pedicure starts at $50 and includes a foot soak, basic nail and cuticle care, a structured gel base and gel polish. Sole and heel callus removal is not included.

06REMOVALIs nail removal complimentary?

Removal of nail work done at Foxie Beauty is complimentary when you have a new nail service with us at the same appointment.

Complimentary removal cannot be booked on its own. Removal-only appointments and removal of another salon’s work are charged at the listed rates.

07LASH STYLESCan you help me choose a lash style?

Of course. Whether you prefer natural, wispy, manga or fuller lashes, we can help you choose a style based on your eye shape and preferred look. You’re welcome to bring a reference photo; individual results may vary.

08PERMANENT MAKEUPWhich permanent makeup services do you offer? Is a touch-up included?

We offer hairstroke brows using microblading, powder brows using micropigmentation, and combo brows combining both techniques. Lip blushing and lash line enhancement are also available.

One complimentary touch-up is included with single-colour powder brows, multi-colour powder brows, combo brows and lip blushing. Manual powder brows do not include a touch-up. Please confirm touch-up inclusions for hairstroke brows and eyeliner when booking.

Questions about cancellations, lateness or repairs? See our Salon Policies.

Booking & Deposits

We operate by appointment. A deposit paid by credit card is required to confirm an online booking. Only the deposit is paid when booking; the remaining balance is due at the salon after your service.

The deposit amount is shown before you complete your booking. Please check your service, date and time before paying.

Running Late?

Please let us know as soon as possible if you’re running late.

  • If you arrive more than 10 minutes late, we may need to simplify your nail design or adjust the service to fit the remaining appointment time.
  • If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment is automatically cancelled and your deposit is forfeited. No remaining service balance is charged.
Cancellations & No-Shows

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment using the link in your booking confirmation.

  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance: your deposit is refundable.
  • Rescheduling at least 24 hours in advance: your deposit transfers to your new appointment.
  • Cancellations or rescheduling less than 24 hours in advance: your deposit is forfeited.
  • No-shows: your deposit is forfeited.

The cancellation charge is limited to the deposit; the remaining service balance is not charged.

Service Concerns & Repairs

If something isn’t right after your visit, please contact us promptly so we can help.

For concerns about the quality of your nail or lash service, contact us within 7 days of your appointment. We offer a complimentary repair for issues with the original service; please include a photo so we can assess the concern and arrange a suitable appointment.

Repairs cover the original service, rather than a change of design or damage from an accident or improper aftercare. Routine lash refills follow the separate refill schedule above. Brow healing and touch-ups follow your technician’s aftercare and follow-up plan.

Deposit & Refund Enquiries

If you cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment, your deposit is refundable. If you reschedule at least 24 hours in advance, your deposit transfers to the new appointment.

For help with a deposit refund or any payment concern, contact us with your booking details. Complimentary repairs and lash refills are handled separately from payment and refund enquiries.
